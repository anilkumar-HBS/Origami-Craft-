Beautiful Paper Swan

Follow these simple steps to create your own paper swan.

Step 1

Take a square piece of paper and place it on a flat surface.

Step 2

Fold the paper diagonally to make a triangle. Press the fold firmly.

Step 3

Fold both sides toward the center line. This creates the basic body shape.

Step 4

Fold the long pointed section upward to form the swan's neck.

Step 5

Make a small fold at the top to create the swan's head.

Step 6

Gently shape the body and wings. Your paper swan is ready!
