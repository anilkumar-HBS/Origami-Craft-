Beautiful Paper Flower

Follow these simple steps to create your own paper flower.

Step 1

Take a square piece of colorful paper and place it on a flat surface.

Step 2

Fold the paper in half and press the fold firmly with your fingers.

Step 3

Fold the paper again to create a smaller square shape.

Step 4

Carefully fold the corners inward to create the flower petals.

Step 5

Gently open the folded sections and shape each petal.

Step 6

Adjust the petals to make the flower look beautiful. Your paper flower is ready!
