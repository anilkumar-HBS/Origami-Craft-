Beautiful Paper Butterfly

Follow these simple steps to create your own paper butterfly.

Step 1

Take a square piece of colorful paper and place it on a flat surface.

Step 2

Fold the paper diagonally and press the fold firmly.

Step 3

Open the paper and fold the other diagonal to create guide lines.

Step 4

Fold the sides toward the center to create the butterfly body.

Step 5

Fold the upper sections outward to form the butterfly wings.

Step 6

Gently shape the wings. Your paper butterfly is ready!
